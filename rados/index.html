What it does

The whole TV, rebuilt.

The whole TV, rebuilt. A fast, ad free, fully customizable home screen, web browser and keyboard for Android TV and Google TV boxes. No accounts, no analytics, no preinstalled junk.

01

A home screen that opens ready

A greeting, clock, weather, scores, calendar and rotating photo sit beside your apps. Saved content appears first, then each card updates from the network.

02

Made for the remote

Hold OK to move an app, the scores or the Up Next card. The home screen remembers your order. Settings brings the clock, weather, display, network and keyboard controls into one glass panel.

03

Rad Browser

Arrow keys move a pointer that speeds up while held and scrolls at the edge. OK clicks. The mic button searches by voice. The browser app is 362 KB.

04

Rad Shield

Ads and trackers are stopped before they download. Element hiding removes leftover boxes, and popup rules stop unwanted windows. A counter shows blocked requests, with a switch for each site.

05

Widgets in your own layout

Clock, weather, scores, calendar, countdown, photo, note and a speed test. Each has three sizes and five styles, and you place them with the remote.

06

Glass Keys

An onscreen keyboard for the whole TV. About 30,000 English words power suggestions and autocorrect. Hold OK for a capital; Delete just after a correction restores what you typed.

How it works

From the first step to the next.

  1. 01

    Power on

    A spinning glass globe and chime fill the seconds Android would otherwise leave blank. The animation runs about six and a half seconds while the home screen loads behind it.

  2. 02

    Open a filled home screen

    The last weather, scores, calendar and photo appear from storage before the network refresh. The box fetches updates itself, so no computer needs to stay on.

  3. 03

    Move with the remote

    The Home screen helper opens radOS directly when you press Home. It watches the Home key and reads no screen content. A long press keeps Google TV’s quick settings behavior.

  4. 04

    Browse and type

    Rad Browser uses Android WebView. Rad Shield checks requests against local filter lists, refreshed every four days. Glass Keys works as the keyboard in every app.

Three small Android apps use the plain Android SDK tools, with no Gradle and no third party libraries. Rad Shield uses EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters, uBlock privacy filters and Peter Lowe’s list. There is no radOS server, no account and no analytics. The box talks directly to weather, scores, photo, calendar and filter services.

MIT licensed. The font, word list and services have their own terms in the repository. radOS is not affiliated with Google, ESPN, Wikimedia, Apple or the other companies named there.

Measured on a real TV

Small apps. Specific results.

These are measurements from one onn 4K Pro with Google TV, Android 14 and 3 GB of memory, not a promise for every box.

  • About 98 MB for the radOS home screen, measured two minutes after a restart on October 19, 2026. Google’s launcher held about 295 MB on the same box, measured October 18.
  • About 26 seconds from restart to the radOS home screen, measured October 18, 2026.
  • Rad Shield scored 100 out of 100 on AdBlock Tester on October 19, 2026: all 11 services and 22 checks.
  • 145,896 filter rules loaded in 185 ms on a Mac. The same October 19 test averaged about 0.19 ms per request over 2,800 checks, with all 14 test cases correct.
  • Three apps: the home screen and Settings, Rad Browser, and Glass Keys. Installed sizes measured October 19 were 2.9 MB, 362 KB and 193 KB.

Other Android TV and Google TV boxes on Android 12 or newer should work but have not been tested. Remote buttons differ between brands.
