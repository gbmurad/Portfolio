What Changed

  1. Prices that cover cost. I set a tiered markup on supplier cost, found 55 variants selling below cost, and repriced them to it, capped at the supplier's retail price.
  2. A faster store. The Brands page looked up every one of 446 brands one at a time and took up to 2.9 seconds to build. The rebuilt page, in the next release, takes about 0.16 seconds.
  3. A calmer front page. The current banner cycles through panels on its own. The new one, in the next release, is a single animated scene that plays once and then rests.
