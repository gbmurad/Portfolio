What it does

Two lines. One store.

Two lines on one Shopify store: designer fragrance, shipped straight from the supplier, and Car Perfume, a small glass bottle of its own that hangs from a rearview mirror.

Each line makes money a different way. Resale earns a markup without holding stock; Car Perfume is a product of our own, built for repeat orders.

The supplier holds the stock and ships each order. Escentials earns the markup between the supplier's cost and its own price, which never goes above the supplier's retail price.

How it works

From the first step to the next.

  1. 01

    Set the catalog and price

    Supplier stock feeds the catalog. A tiered markup on supplier cost sets the price, capped at the supplier’s retail price.

  2. 02

    Choose a product

    Customers shop designer fragrance or choose one of 14 Car Perfume scents and ten bottle colors. The sales channels are Shopify and eBay.

  3. 03

    Fill the order

    The supplier ships designer fragrance straight to the customer. Car Perfume customers can return for refills in 10, 20 and 30 ml.

  4. 04

    Read the results

    The books bring together FY2025 statements, budget versus actual, a SQL customer database and a Tableau dashboard.

A real business, kept apart from the practice cases. Its books use illustrative figures for privacy.

My part

What I bring to it.

I set the catalog, the prices and the margins, manage supplier stock, keep the books, and direct the website rebuild.

  1. Pricing. I set a tiered markup on supplier cost, capped at the supplier's retail price, found 55 variants selling below cost, and repriced them.
  2. The catalog. 5,678 active products from 446 brands, sorted into categories and brand pages, with stock synced from the supplier.
  3. Car Perfume. The store's own line: 14 scents in ten bottle colors, with refills in 10, 20 and 30 ml and a deal on each.
  4. The website. I direct the rebuild: a category wheel, a Brands page that builds in about 0.16 seconds instead of up to 2.9 (in the next release), and the live 3D Car Perfume above.
  5. The books. FY2025 statements, budget versus actual, a SQL customer database and a Tableau dashboard, on their own page.

Books kept for FY2025. The website rebuild began in September 2026.
