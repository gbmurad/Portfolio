What it does

Everything in its place.

Four tabs bring it together: Progress, Read, Notes and Learn. Named reading trackers, chapter and verse notes, daily devotionals, trivia and a home screen widget you color yourself. Free, with no ads, no purchases and no tracking. For iPhone with iOS 26 and newer.

01

Progress

A morning greeting, Verse of the Day, today's devotional and a prayer card. Name your reading trackers, like Me, Study or Church, and follow chapters read out of 1,189, books finished, and Old and New Testament progress.

02

Read

Continue Reading where you left off, or Read by Section: Law, History, Wisdom and Poetry, and more. See every book with its progress. Highlight in the reader, choose light or dark, and mark a chapter as read.

03

Notes

Keep notes for any chapter or verse, with your own tags. Find them grouped by Today, Yesterday and This Week.

04

Learn

Daily Devotional, Daily Challenge, Trivia with 361 questions, Finish the Verse, The Books and 98 Fun Facts.

How it works

From the first step to the next.

  1. 01

    Choose a tracker

    Name your reading trackers, like Me, Study or Church, and follow chapters, books and Testament progress.

  2. 02

    Pick up your reading

    Continue Reading where you left off, read by section, or find a chapter with a reference like john 3.

  3. 03

    Keep what stands out

    Highlight in the reader and keep notes for any chapter or verse, organized with your own tags.

  4. 04

    Return and learn

    Mark a chapter as read, see your progress, and come back for a devotional, trivia or a daily challenge.
