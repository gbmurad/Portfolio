What it does

A quieter web.

An ad and tracker blocker in two forms: an app that protects Safari on iPhone, and a Chrome extension. Both use the same public filter lists as Rad Browser on radOS: EasyList, EasyPrivacy, the uBlock Origin lists and Peter Lowe's list.

01

Quiet pages

Ads and trackers are blocked on every site, and the empty boxes ads leave behind are hidden.

02

Pop ups stopped first

New windows and tabs a page opens behind your back are stopped before they appear. Invisible click catchers that hijack your first click are switched off.

03

Three strengths

Lite is ads only. Balanced is ads and trackers. Strict adds cookie banners, social widgets and annoyances. Every category has its own switch.

04

The same controls on both

On iPhone the strengths, switches, Blocked list and My blocks live right in the app. In Chrome they live in one popup.

05

YouTube ads in Chrome

Ad slots are hidden, and any ad that still gets through is skipped. Videos can take a few seconds longer to start, because the ad data is removed before the player reads it.

06

Private by design

No account, no analytics and no server of ours. The only network traffic is downloading the public filter lists.

How it works

From the first step to the next.

  1. 01

    Turn it on

    On iPhone, turn on the Safari extension in Settings. In Chrome, add the extension and give it about half a minute to download the lists and build its rules.

  2. 02

    Browse as usual

    Pages load without the ads, and the Blocked list shows what was stopped.

  3. 03

    Adjust a site

    If a site breaks, turn the shield off for that site and reload.

  4. 04

    Stay up to date

    Lists refresh by themselves. A failed download keeps the last good rules.

The iPhone app is a SwiftUI app around a Safari Web Extension, and the two share settings and status through an App Group. Both versions turn the filter lists into the browser's own blocking rules, so your browsing stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
