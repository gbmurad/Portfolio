How I work

Curiosity in.
Clarity out.

The same five steps behind every case study here, and behind the work I would do for you.

  1. 01Pull the DataTransactions into a database, shaped with SQL and checked against the source.
  2. 02Build the ModelThree linked statements in Excel, with every input in one place.
  3. 03Make It TieChecks on every statement, so an error shows up before anyone reads the report.
  4. 04Find the StoryVariances, trends and ratios that explain what changed and why.
  5. 05Present ItA short memo, a dashboard or a deck that someone outside finance can follow.
