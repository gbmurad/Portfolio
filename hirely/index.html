What it does

Less busywork. More moving forward.

Your whole job search, at home on your iPhone. Find jobs, shape a draft from your own résumé, and follow each application from To apply to Applied, Interviewing and Offer.

01

Find your next possibility

Search public job boards from 50+ companies on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby. Save a search for each kind of role, with filters for place, remote or hybrid work, pay, job type and posting age.

02

A first draft, already yours

Auto Apply writes a tailored cover letter and answers from your own résumé, without inventing experience. Edit it yourself or ask for shorter or more formal wording. You review. You submit.

03

Your résumé, still your résumé

Keep your Word résumé’s exact look in previews and PDFs. Get a résumé review with fixes, then make the draft your own.

04

Every application in view

Move cards through To apply, Applied, Interviewing and Offer. See your response rate, build a daily streak and keep follow up reminders in one place.

05

A reminder at the right time

Your phone schedules a reminder two weeks after applying, and on the morning a next step is due.

06

Your search stays yours

Your board and documents stay on your iPhone. When you ask for AI help, the content needed to write or review is sent for processing. No ads. No tracking across apps.

How it works

From the first step to the next.

  1. 01

    Sign in and find a role

    Sign in with Apple. Search the public job boards and save jobs to your To apply queue.

  2. 02

    Make the draft yours

    Start with your résumé. Review the tailored letter and answers, then make your edits.

  3. 03

    Apply yourself

    Tap Apply to open the posting. When you close it, Hirely asks “Did you apply?” Yes moves the card to Applied.

  4. 04

    Keep the next step in sight

    Your phone schedules the reminders. When a company replies, swipe the job to its next stage.

The SwiftUI app works with a small Node server for Sign in with Apple, a REST API and App Store subscription checks. Its MCP endpoint lets Claude list, add and update jobs, mark them applied and list responses to help fill the queue. Claude never submits applications; you apply.
